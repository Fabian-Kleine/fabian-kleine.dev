## 3. Hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Avenue #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA ("Vercel").

When you visit the website, your browser automatically transmits data that Vercel processes in server log files. This includes:

- your IP address,
- the date and time of the request,
- the requested page or file,
- the referrer URL (the page you came from),
- the browser type and version, and the operating system.

This data is required to deliver the website and to keep it stable and secure (for example to detect and defend against attacks). The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is the secure and reliable provision of this website.

I have concluded a data processing agreement with Vercel (Art. 28 GDPR). Vercel may also process data in the USA. Vercel is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition, Vercel's data processing agreement includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 4. Web Analytics

This website uses Vercel Web Analytics to collect anonymous statistics on how the website is used. The analytics script is served from this website's own domain.

For each page view, the following data may be recorded:

- the time of the page view,
- the visited page and the referrer,
- the approximate location (country, region, city), derived from your IP address,
- the operating system, browser, and device type (desktop, mobile, tablet).

Vercel Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not store anything on your device. To count visitors, a hash is calculated from the incoming request. This hash is discarded after 24 hours and cannot be used to recognize you across websites or over a longer period. Your IP address is not stored. I only see aggregated statistics.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to understand which content is of interest and to improve the website. You can object to this processing at any time (see section 9).

The recipient and the third-country transfer are the same as described in section 3 (Vercel Inc., USA, EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework).

## 5. Contact Form

If you send me a message via the contact form, the following data is processed:

- your name,
- your email address,
- the subject and the content of your message.

The data is transmitted encrypted to this website's server (Vercel, see section 3) and sent to me by email using the email delivery service Resend, operated by Plus Five Five, Inc., USA ("Resend"). I have concluded a data processing agreement with Resend (Art. 28 GDPR). Resend is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so the transfer to the USA is based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

I use your data only to process and answer your inquiry. It is not shared with anyone else.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your inquiry relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures (for example a project request). In all other cases, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is to answer inquiries addressed to me.

Providing this data is voluntary. Without it, I cannot answer your inquiry.

I delete your data once your inquiry has been fully dealt with, unless statutory retention obligations (for example under commercial or tax law) require me to keep it longer.

## 6. External Media (Project Videos)

Some project showcase videos are not hosted on this website, but by the following providers:

- **jsDelivr**, operated by Volentio JSD Limited, a company registered in England and Wales. jsDelivr uses third-party networks (for example Cloudflare) to deliver files, which may also process data outside the EU and the United Kingdom.
- **GitHub**, operated by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA.

When a video is loaded, your browser connects directly to these providers. They receive your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested file, the referrer URL, and information about your browser. According to my checks at the time of this policy, no cookies are set when the videos are loaded.

Videos from these providers are only loaded after you have given your consent in the banner ("Allow videos"). Without consent, only preview images hosted on this website are shown.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act). Data transfers to the United Kingdom are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision for the United Kingdom. GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Where a transfer to a third country is not covered by an adequacy decision, it is based on your explicit consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR). In such countries, authorities may be able to access the data, and you may not be able to enforce your rights effectively.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future by clicking "Privacy settings" in the footer and choosing "Images only". Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (see the <TypographyLink target="_self" href="/legal/cookie-policy">Cookie Policy</TypographyLink>).

For more information, see the privacy policies of [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy) and [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 9. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- right of access (Art. 15 GDPR),
- right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR),
- right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR),
- right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
- right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
- right to withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

**Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR):** Where processing is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object to it at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. I will then no longer process your data unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

To exercise your rights, contact me using the details in section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, your place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

There is no automated decision-making, including profiling (Art. 22 GDPR).
