# Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This page explains which information this website stores on your device. The short version: there are no tracking or advertising cookies, and everything listed below is either necessary for a function you have used or records your own privacy choice.

## What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. Local storage and session storage are similar browser features that let a website keep small amounts of data on your device. Unlike cookies, data in local or session storage is never sent to a server automatically.

## What This Website Stores

### NEXT_LOCALE (cookie)

- **Purpose:** Remembers the language you selected with the language switcher (English or German).
- **When it is set:** Only when you switch the language. Until then, the language is chosen based on your browser settings.
- **Duration:** 1 year.
- **Provider:** This website (first-party). It is sent to this website's server to display pages in your language.

### kleine-os-consent (local storage)

- **Purpose:** Stores your decision whether project videos from external providers (jsDelivr, GitHub) may be loaded.
- **When it is set:** When you click "Allow videos" or "Images only" in the banner.
- **Duration:** Until you change your decision via "Privacy settings" in the footer or clear your browser data.
- **Provider:** This website. It never leaves your device.

### kleine-os-booted (session storage)

- **Purpose:** Remembers that the start animation has already been shown, so it does not play again on every page.
- **Duration:** Until you close the browser tab.
- **Provider:** This website. It never leaves your device.

## Legal Basis

All entries listed above are strictly necessary to provide a function you have explicitly requested (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). No consent is therefore required to store them. Any further processing of personal data is described in the <TypographyLink target="_self" href="/legal/privacy-policy">Privacy Policy</TypographyLink>.

## Analytics Without Cookies

This website uses Vercel Web Analytics for anonymous visitor statistics. It does not set cookies and does not store anything on your device. Details are in section 4 of the <TypographyLink target="_self" href="/legal/privacy-policy">Privacy Policy</TypographyLink>.

## External Media

If you allow project videos, your browser loads them directly from jsDelivr and GitHub. According to my checks at the time of this policy, these providers do not set cookies when the videos are loaded. They do receive your IP address. See section 6 of the <TypographyLink target="_self" href="/legal/privacy-policy">Privacy Policy</TypographyLink>.

## Managing Your Settings

- To change or withdraw your decision about external media, click "Privacy settings" in the footer.
- You can view and delete cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings. If you delete the `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie, the website falls back to your browser's language. If you delete `kleine-os-consent`, you will be asked about external media again.

I update this policy when the website changes. The current version is always available on this page.
